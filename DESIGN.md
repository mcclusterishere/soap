# Destined Skinn — Design Direction

## Product goal
Turn a link-in-bio presence into a mobile-first brand storefront that feels like an app, while keeping every live purchase/social destination reachable through the current Linktree until the final commerce URLs are supplied.

## Current visual system
- Editorial serif headlines paired with restrained sans/mono utility text.
- Warm skin-tone palette instead of generic pink beauty branding.
- Oversized art-directed product forms made in CSS so the prototype has no stock-image dependency.
- Desktop editorial layout collapses into an app-like mobile experience with persistent bottom navigation.
- Slide-over menu, sticky navigation, installable web app manifest, and offline shell.

## Content status
The supplied Linktree URL was not readable by the research fetcher during this build, so outbound CTAs intentionally route to the current Linktree instead of inventing checkout, social, or product URLs. Public search results support Destined Skinn as a Georgia skincare business and identify soap bars in its public product story. Final product names, prices, photography, social URLs, fulfillment details, policies, and brand-approved copy should replace the provisional presentation copy before production launch.
